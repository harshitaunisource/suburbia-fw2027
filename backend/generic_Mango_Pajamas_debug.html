Comfort and style with women’s pyjamas from Mango

There’s nothing better than sleeping in a comfortable pyjama, right? And that sense of comfort is essential for getting a good rest.

Women’s pyjamas: rest without giving up style

That’s exactly why, at Mango, our top priority with our women’s pyjamas is: the softness and comfort of the fabrics we use to make the pyjama shirts, T-shirts and trousers.

Winter pyjamas: warmth and comfort

Check design, striped, satin finishes... Choose your favourite women’s pyjama from this carefully curated selection. Here you’ll find both winter pyjamas and summer pyjamas.

Summer pyjamas: coolness and softness

We have women’s satin long-sleeve shirt-style pyjamas, for the colder months, so you can sleep comfortably without feeling chilly.

Versatile sets for every occasion

What’s more, we offer a selection of jumpers, cardigans and sweatshirts for the colder months. We also have pyjama sets with an pyjama T-shirt, in short or long sleeves, and women’s pyjama trousers, fluid or tailored. We have a women’s pyjama for every style. Choose a versatile pyjama trousers format, halfway between a tracksuit and pyjamas, and pair it with a pretty cardigan for an easy yet polished look.

How to style pyjamas for casual looks

Or if you’re feeling bold, you can do what celebrities and street-style icons do and head out for dinner in a sophisticated satin pyjama with heeled sandals. Or in a more understated version, you can use the pyjama shirts as a blouse.
